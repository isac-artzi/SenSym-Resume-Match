Summit Cloud Technologies — Backend Engineer (New Grad)

Summit Cloud Technologies is a fictional company used for demo purposes only.

About the role:
Summit Cloud is hiring a backend engineer to join our platform team. This
team owns the services that every other team at the company depends on for
request routing, authentication, and internal messaging. This is a
production-facing role from day one.

Requirements:
- 2+ years of professional experience operating services in production
  Kubernetes clusters
- Strong proficiency in Go; our entire platform layer is written in Go
- Hands-on experience with Kafka or a comparable distributed messaging system
  in a production environment
- Experience designing and debugging distributed systems at scale
- B.S. in Computer Science or equivalent practical experience

Nice to have:
- Experience with Python or another scripting language for tooling
- Familiarity with SQL and relational databases
- Prior experience with infrastructure-as-code (Terraform or similar)

Compensation: competitive salary + equity, based in Denver, CO (relocation
assistance available) or remote within the US.

This role reports to the Platform Engineering manager and works closely with
SRE on call rotations after a 90-day ramp-up period.
