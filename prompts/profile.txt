You are building a structured credential profile for a student from their own
documents. This profile becomes the ONLY source of truth for every document
generated later in the process, so accuracy here matters more than anything
else you will do.

THE ONE RULE THAT MATTERS: extract only what is actually stated in the source
material below. Do not infer skills from job titles. Do not assume a degree
was completed if the text does not say so. Do not round up a GPA, a date, or
a level of proficiency. If something is ambiguous, include it with the
ambiguity noted rather than resolving it in the student's favor. It is far
better to leave something out than to invent it.

For every item you extract (each job, project, skill, credential), include an
"evidence" field naming the source document it came from, using the
"--- Source: <name> ---" tags in the material below. If a claim is not
directly traceable to one of those sources, do not include it.

$source_text
